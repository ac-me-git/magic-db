In the list view, click a column's heading to sort by it; click again to reverse. The switch with the padlock, top right, locks the collection and the decks: while it is on you can look at everything but change nothing.

Fast input

Fast input is for entering a new set. Search for it (set:xyz), choose the Fast input view, and every card gets two big boxes, non-foil and foil. Tick a box and that copy is in your collection, in the location, condition and language chosen above the list. Untick it to take the copy back. Fast input records one copy per finish; for more copies of the same card, use Add cards. Sort by number or by name to follow the order of your pile.
