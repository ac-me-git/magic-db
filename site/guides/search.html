Search syntax

Type words to search card names, and add filters to narrow the results. The same page is inside the app, under Syntax, with every example clickable. The filter panel on the home page writes these queries for you.

The basics

You typeYou get
lightning boltCards whose name contains both words
"serra angel"Quotes keep words together
!"Lightning Bolt"An exclamation mark asks for the exact name
t:dragon c:rFilters are written key:value, and all of them must match
t:elf or t:goblinEither one or the other
(t:elf or t:goblin) mv<=2Parentheses group, as in arithmetic
c:r -t:creatureA minus sign excludes

Number filters accept : = != < <= > >=, as in mv<=3 or pow>=5. Upper and lower case do not matter.

Colours

Write any combination of w u b r g, a colour's name, c for colourless or m for multicolour.

You typeYou get
c:rgCards that are at least red and green
c=rgExactly red and green
c<=rgRed, green, both, or colourless
c:cColourless cards
c:mCards of two or more colours
id:wubCards that fit in a white-blue-black Commander deck

One result per card, or per printing

A search normally shows each card once, with the printing that matches best. Add unique:prints to see every printing. Tokens and other extras are hidden unless you ask for a set, a collector number, or add include:extras.

Your collection in a search

have: is about the card, whatever the printing; haveprint:, qty, loc:, cond:, finish: and lang: are about the exact printing shown. So set:mh3 haveprint:no unique:prints lists the printings of a set you are missing, and have:yes t:dragon every dragon you own in any printing.

Limits

A search can be at most 2,000 characters and 100 terms long.
