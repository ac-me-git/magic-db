Another Magic Tracking Gizmo

A self-hosted web app to browse every Magic: The Gathering card and keep track of your own collection and decks. Runs on a Windows PC with nothing else to install, or in Docker. Use it from any browser, phone included.

Download for Windows Docker Source

Free and open source. No account, no subscription, no telemetry.

Every card, every night

All cards ever printed, plus previewed ones, refreshed nightly from Scryfall.

Search that speaks Magic

Type t:dragon c:r mv<=4 or use the filter panel, which writes the query for you.

Your collection, located

Quantity, finish, condition, language, and where the card physically is: binder, box, deck.

Decks that know what you own

Paste a list and see completeness, missing cards, and cards several decks fight over.

Statistics

By colour, rarity, type and year; value; set completion.

Your data stays yours

One SQLite file, exported to CSV or JSON any time, backed up every night.

A safety lock

Browse on your phone without changing anything by accident.

English and Italian

Switch language at the bottom of any page.

See it at work

Private by design

AMTG runs on your own computer or server. The only things it asks the internet for are card data and images from Scryfall and, on the Administration page, whether GitHub has a newer version (you can switch that off). Your collection never leaves your machine.

Get started

  1. Download magic-db.exe and put it in a folder of its own.
  2. Double-click it. Your browser opens on http://127.0.0.1:8080.
  3. Wait a few minutes while the first start downloads the card catalog (about 80 MB).

On Linux, macOS or a NAS, use Docker. Then read the guides.
